# Privacy Policy for Zeph's Novel Outliner

**Effective Date:** September 29, 2026

Zeph's Novel Outliner is developed and published by **Healthy Mind Media**.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android application **Zeph's Novel Outliner** handles user information.

**App Name:** Zeph's Novel Outliner  
**Package Name:** `com.healthymindmedia.noveloutliner`

## 1. Your Writing Data

Zeph's Novel Outliner is designed so that your novels and writing remain private.

Your content, including novel projects, outlines, scenes and sequels, character profiles, Goal, Motivation and Conflict (GMC) information, notes, and manuscript text, is stored locally on your Android device.

Healthy Mind Media does not operate a server that receives or stores your manuscript, outline, character, or project content.

## 2. Personal Data Collection

Healthy Mind Media does not directly collect, sell, rent, or use your manuscript content for advertising or profiling.

The app does not require a Healthy Mind Media account to create or manage local writing projects.

## 3. Google Drive Backup

Zeph's Novel Outliner may include an optional Google Drive backup or restore feature.

Google Drive backup is used only when you choose to initiate it.

When you use this feature, the app may access the Google services necessary to create, retrieve, or manage the application's backup data in your own Google Drive account.

Your backup data is transferred between your device and your Google Drive storage using Google services.

Healthy Mind Media does not maintain a separate copy of your Google Drive backups on its own servers.

Your use of Google Drive is subject to Google's applicable privacy policy and terms of service.

## 4. Google Play Purchases

Zeph's Novel Outliner may offer an optional paid Pro upgrade through Google Play.

Purchases are processed through **Google Play Billing**.

Healthy Mind Media does not receive or store your complete payment-card information.

The application may receive information from Google Play necessary to determine whether a purchase has been completed and whether Pro features should be unlocked or restored.

Payment-related information processed by Google is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

## 5. Exported Files

The application may allow you to export your writing to formats such as PDF and Microsoft Word (`.docx`).

Exports are created only when you request them.

Exported files are saved or shared to a location selected by you using Android's file or sharing features.

Healthy Mind Media does not automatically receive copies of exported files.

## 6. Writing Reminders

Zeph's Novel Outliner may allow you to schedule writing reminders.

Reminder settings, such as the selected reminder time, are used on your device to provide notifications.

These reminder settings are not transmitted to Healthy Mind Media servers.

## 7. Advertising

Zeph's Novel Outliner does not display third-party advertising.

The application may contain an optional paid upgrade purchased through Google Play.

## 8. Analytics and Tracking

Healthy Mind Media does not use your manuscript content for advertising, behavioral profiling, or tracking.

If future versions of the application introduce analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or other data-processing services, this Privacy Policy and the application's Google Play Data Safety information will be updated accordingly.

## 9. Third-Party Services

Depending on which optional features you use, Zeph's Novel Outliner may interact with Google services, including:

- Google Play
- Google Play Billing
- Google Drive
- Google account authentication services

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Healthy Mind Media does not control Google's independent data-processing practices.

## 10. Data Sharing

Healthy Mind Media does not sell or rent user data.

Your writing content is not shared with Healthy Mind Media or third parties by default.

Information may be sent to a third-party service only when you deliberately use a feature that requires that service, such as Google Drive backup or Android file sharing.

## 11. Data Security

Locally stored application data is protected by the security mechanisms provided by your Android device and operating system.

Information transferred to Google services is handled using the security mechanisms provided by Android and Google.

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 12. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your novel projects and related data are normally stored locally on your device, Healthy Mind Media does not retain a server-side copy of that content.

You may delete projects and other content through the application's available controls.

You may also remove locally stored application data by deleting projects within the app, clearing the application's storage through Android settings, or uninstalling the application.

Files you export or back up outside the application may remain in the destination where you saved them. For example, Google Drive backup files may remain in your Google account until you delete them.

## 13. Children's Privacy

Zeph's Novel Outliner is a general-purpose writing and productivity application.

It is not specifically designed or directed toward children under the age of 13.

Healthy Mind Media does not knowingly collect children's personal information through its own servers.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page and the effective date will be revised.
